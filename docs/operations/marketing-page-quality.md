# Marketing page quality

- Use short, familiar words at a fourth- or fifth-grade reading level. Keep the main offer clear and affirmative.
- Lead with the child's village. Tooth Fairy Network is the product name. The parent chooses who joins and controls the wallet for the child; adults do not jointly own a pooled fund.
- Keep approved childlike artwork purposeful and uncropped. Retain concrete printed Proof, picture/message, certificate and digital-wallet visuals. State the actual number of items included.
- Established FAQs and legal terms require owner approval to rewrite. Record audit questions separately; they do not authorize copy changes or block an approved visual release.
- Preserve approved prices, gift amounts, optional honest-feedback terms, privacy and consent. Do not invent reviews, urgency, returns, guaranteed purchasing power or lifetime value.
- Use the established Dad/Tanda references and owner-selected presenter/voice. New video needs owner approval of the final edit; never embed an obsolete cut or claim listening/lip-sync checks that were not performed. Playback starts only when the visitor chooses it, with controls and captions.
- Keep public canonicals, robots and sitemaps correct while preserving private-route protections. Indexability does not prove Google index coverage.
