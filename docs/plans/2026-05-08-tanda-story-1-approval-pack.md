## Core Direction

Story 1 explains why Tanda's promise now needs a Network.

Tanda is not failing because she misses teeth. She is failing because she is so reliable at the visible ritual that she has started treating the invisible part as "later." Tooth collected, coin left, route complete. But the child's own detail is the thing that makes the tooth belong to them.

The Network exists to protect that child-specific part.

## Story Spine

Working title: **Tanda's First Rule**

Tanda is excellent, stretched thin, and proud of never missing a tooth. A modern child, Elias, writes a funny, imperfect note about losing his tooth on corn. Tanda almost skips the note because another window is calling. The Old Forgetting appears as a small fussy shadow that nibbles away the unnamed detail, making Elias's tooth glow more generically.

Tanda stops racing, reads Elias's exact words, and recovers the memory. Then she opens her satchel and sees the real wound: too many unread teeth are dim. Her leadership turn is not "I need workers." It is "the promise needs more guardians."

First rule:

> No tooth without its story. No child as coin-and-done.

## 32-Frame Production Storyboard

1. Cover - Tanda above the world, Toothlight threads glowing, one tiny shadow bite at the edge.
2. Dinner Is Already Late - Elias's family at the table, ordinary chaos, corn on the plate.
3. The Tooth Comes Out - Elias announces, "I meant to do that," while everyone freezes.
4. The Tooth Words - Dad offers to help write the note; Elias refuses.
5. Bad Spelling, True Story - Elias writes the corn/brave/surprised note with a tiny drawing.
6. Under The Pillow - Tooth, note, drawing, stuffed toy, lights out.
7. Tanda's Route - Tanda moves across glowing windows with impressive speed.
8. Coin And Done - She completes several stops too quickly: tooth taken, coin left.
9. The Unread Pocket - Her satchel has a pocket labeled only by habit: "later."
10. A Dull Glow - One unread tooth loses its child-specific color.
11. Professional Entrance - Tanda enters Elias's room beautifully, bumps the planet mobile.
12. The Warm Tooth - Elias's tooth glows with dinner laughter.
13. Another Window Calls - Tanda almost moves on before reading the note.
14. I'll Read It After - Her flaw lands: she still cares, but she is postponing care.
15. The Note Slips - Elias's note falls half-hidden beside the bed.
16. The First Nibble - A tiny shadow nibbles the corn drawing.
17. Don't Forget The Corn Part - Elias half-wakes and mumbles the exact detail.
18. Too Plain - The tooth's glow becomes generic: child, dinner, tooth, but no Elias.
19. The Voice - "Unread things are unattended things."
20. Memory Opens - Corn crunch, Dad's worried face, Elias checking for blood, then pride.
21. The Missing Bit - The funniest, truest detail starts vanishing first.
22. Tanda Gives Chase - She dives through golden memory threads inside the Toothlight.
23. Exact Words Work - Tanda reads Elias's note aloud, spelling mistakes and all.
24. The Old Forgetting Retreats - It spits out the detail, annoyed: "Too much corn."
25. The First Toothlight - Tooth, note, drawing, and memory fuse into a kept thing.
26. The Real Wound - Tanda opens her satchel: too many unread teeth are dim.
27. The Old Promise - Her pendant glows with Story 2's promise: notice what was outgrown.
28. The First Rule - "No tooth without its story. No child as coin-and-done."
29. Letter To Perez - A golden invitation reaches a tiny Madrid door.
30. Letters Elsewhere - Rooftop song, sunlit threshold, quiet path, feathered perch.
31. Morning Proof - Elias wakes to a coin and tiny note: Tanda kept the corn part.
32. CTA / Network Begins - Tanda leads the first living Network, with Elias's Toothlight glowing at the center.
