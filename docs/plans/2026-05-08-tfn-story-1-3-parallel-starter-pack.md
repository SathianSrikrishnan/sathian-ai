Current source:

- `public/story-assets/tanda/f-09-keeping.png`
- `public/story-assets/tanda/f-10-return.png`

Creative job:

Make this the modern flagship story. It should introduce Tanda as the leader/anchor collector who is overwhelmed because children are losing teeth faster than one fairy can honor properly. This is not about app mechanics. It is about responsibility, ownership, and asking for help before something precious gets missed.

Child protagonist:

Use one clear child, age 5-7. The child should have an ordinary bedtime-world problem that mirrors Tanda's larger problem: wanting to do it alone, wanting control, or worrying that asking for help means failing.

Tanda arc:

She begins capable but overloaded. She misses or nearly misses a child, feels the weight of that, and realizes the promise from Story 2 was never meant to make her carry everything alone. By the end, she writes or sends the first invitations to other collectors.

Target:

24-32 frames. Keep it warm, readable, and emotionally direct. Avoid corporate "network" language. The network is felt through Toothlight, not explained.

- `public/story-assets/perez/pz-05-rooftop.jpg`

Creative job:

Make this the first real collector relationship story. Tanda must have a reason to visit Perez, and Perez must have his own way, pride, competence, and problem. They should not simply agree. The story should let them learn from each other.

Child protagonist:

Use a Madrid child, age 5-7, with a small but real emotional problem. The lost tooth tradition should intersect naturally with family life: pillow, note, mouse collector, city at night, maybe bakery warmth.

Collector conflict:

Perez is good at the work and a little too proud of his system. Tanda sees the larger network need but should not steamroll him. The lesson is humility, not obedience.

Target:

24-32 frames. More humor and movement than Story 2. Keep it charming, clever, and specific. Tanda and Perez should have chemistry: two professionals with different scales, different habits, and mutual respect by the end.

## Prompt To Paste Into Each Parallel Chat

Use this repo and the Story 2 approved baseline as reference. Read:

Then produce the next story in the same production pattern:

- approve the story spine first,
- create a 24-32 frame storyboard,
- generate or select one unique image per frame,
- preserve character consistency,
- write the final story data,
- build a full-image HTML preview,
- run checks before calling it ready.

Do not copy the Story 2 plot. Copy its production standard.
