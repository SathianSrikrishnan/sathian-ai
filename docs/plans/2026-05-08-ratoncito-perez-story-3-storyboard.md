## Story Spine

Lucia, a six-year-old in Madrid, loses her first tooth in a household with two true tooth traditions. Papa grew up with Ratoncito Perez. Mom grew up with the Tooth Fairy. Lucia does not want to choose one parent story over the other, so she leaves a note under her pillow: "Can both of you come?"

The tooth calls both collectors. Perez answers through mouse law: routes, ledgers, hidden doors, coins, stamps, family duty, and local trust. Tanda answers through Toothlight: the feeling inside the tooth and the family memories it carries. Perez fears Tanda's larger fairy network will make mouse traditions decorative and small. Tanda fears Perez's strict code will protect the tooth while missing Lucia's deeper question.

When both try to collect, the tooth will not move. Its light splits into two true claims. The resolution is not one collector winning. Perez carries the tooth through Madrid's local path; Tanda carries the memory through Toothlight. Lucia wakes with a coin, a tiny stamped receipt, and proof that both family stories came.
