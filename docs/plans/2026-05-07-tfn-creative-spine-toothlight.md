# TFN Creative Spine: Toothlight

Status: locked working direction
Date: 2026-05-07

## Core Spine

A child loses something small, adds something of themselves to it, and learns that what is theirs is worth caring for.

The Tooth Fairy Network exists because children's small things are not small to them.

## Story Engine

Default story pattern:

- Child: a local child with a real childhood problem.
- Family anchor: parent, grandparent, godparent, sibling, teacher, or friend.
- Collector: the magical keeper whose flaw mirrors the child's problem.
- Ritual: the tooth tradition forces a choice.
- Network echo: one small sensory flash of another child's world.
- Gift: the child adds something of themselves: note, drawing, song, photo, name, object, or phrase.

The stories are standalone bedtime stories first. The network is a reward for rereading, not homework.

## Toothlight

Toothlight is TFN language for the created keepsake.

A Toothlight can include:

- a photo of the tooth
- the child's drawing, note, name, phrase, or spoken memory
- an AI-enhanced Collector Style / Network Style
- family contributions over time
- the permanent record the child can own later

Toothlight is broad enough to cover image, story, enhancement, and deposit without sounding like finance.

## Tanda's Magic

Tanda's magic comes from a promise.

The first tooth fee was not payment. It was a promise to care for what the child had outgrown.

In the Norse story, Tanda is the child. Her father carries her tooth. His defining choice is mercy. The tooth does not give him power to win. It reminds him what he is responsible for. He shows mercy when he could destroy someone, and that mercy transforms the tooth fee into a promise.

That promise becomes Tanda's magic:

She can carry what children outgrow without letting it disappear.

## Flagship Three

1. Tanda's First Night
   - Modern network origin.
   - Tanda is overwhelmed and skipping the meaningful part.
   - Lesson: asking for help is responsibility.

2. Tanda Fae and the Tooth Fee
   - Mythic Norse origin.
   - Tanda is the child; her father carries her tooth.
   - Lesson: courage is care under pressure; mercy is strength.

3. Perez and the Sealed Window
   - Spain / Ratoncito Perez.
   - A proud collector misses one tooth because the world changed and he would not ask for help.
   - Lesson: humility; mistakes do not break you.
