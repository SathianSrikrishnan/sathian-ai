## LinkedIn — recommended primary post

“Why does Saraswati carry music?” one of my seven-year-old daughters asked me.

I was born Hindu. It took my children to make me study what I had inherited.

That question became a test I now use on technology:

Does it deepen human capability?

Does it create durable flourishing?

I call them the Saraswati test and the Lakshmi test. I have been trying them on
AI, crypto, the Solana Observatory, and the products I am building for families.

This is not a claim that software is divine. It is a refusal to let one kind of
power excuse the absence of another. Intelligence without judgment is
dangerous. Wealth without flourishing is hollow.
