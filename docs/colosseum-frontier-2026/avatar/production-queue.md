## Step 1 - CEO Portrait Still

Output needed:

- One waist-up Tanda portrait for lip sync.
- 16:9 canvas or portrait subject with enough padding to crop into 16:9 and 9:16.
- Soft background.

Prompt:

```text
Create Tanda, the Tooth Fairy Network CEO, matching the provided young brunette fairy reference. Preserve her warm brown wavy hair, large expressive brown eyes, friendly youthful face, white layered dress, small leather satchel, and pastel iridescent wings. Waist-up talking-avatar portrait, looking directly at camera with calm founder confidence and a gentle smile. Polished cinematic 3D character render, soft warm studio lighting, simple pale background, mouth clearly visible for lip-sync animation. No text, no logo, no extra characters, no old or silver-haired version, no watercolor style.
```

Acceptance:

- Face matches the current app Tanda.
- Mouth is not blocked.
- Eyes are forward and stable.
- No extra props in front of the face.

## Step 2 - Ten-Second Avatar Test

Line:

"I am Tanda, CEO of the Tooth Fairy Network. We turn a lost tooth into a keepsake, a family gift, and a child's first Solana savings story."

Acceptance:

- Face consistency survives motion.
- Mouth movement is good enough for a pitch.
- Voice feels warm, clear, and credible.
- Background does not distract.
