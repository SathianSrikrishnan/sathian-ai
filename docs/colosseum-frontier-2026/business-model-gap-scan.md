# Business Model Gap Scan

Purpose: identify any missing pitch angles before final storyboarding.

## Already Strong

### Distributed Family

This is a strong wedge. Modern families are spread across homes, cities, and caregiving arrangements. TFN gives them a simple shared ritual around a child's milestone.

Pitch line:

> Families are more distributed than ever. Tooth Fairy Network gives them one small ritual where everyone can still show up.

### Sticky Solana Accounts

This is a strong ecosystem line. TFN gives non-trader families a reason to enter Solana and keep value there.

Pitch line:

> TFN turns family milestones into sticky Solana accounts.

### Character-Led Trust

Tanda helps the product feel safe and legible without making it feel like finance first.

Pitch line:

> Tanda makes the technology feel like a family ritual instead of a crypto transaction.

## Missing Or Underused Angles

### 1. Trust And Custody Clarity

Parents will care who controls the money. Judges will care that this is not just a cute wrapper around custodial balances.

Add to technical video:

> Family deposits are enforced by Solana account state and program logic, not just a balance in our database.

Add lightly to pitch:

> The family gift is held by the program, not by the Tooth Fairy.

### 2. Fiat On-Ramp Conversion

MoonPay matters because non-crypto relatives are the growth loop. If Grandma can use a card and the value lands in Solana, the product bridges the real world into the ecosystem.

Use only if working:

> A relative can start with a card and still create a Solana-native gift.

### 3. Family Graph As A Network Effect

The network is not just many children. It is the family graph around each child.

Each keepsake can pull in:

- Parent.
- Co-parent.
- Grandparent.
- Aunt/uncle.
- Close family friend.
- Later: dental office, school, cultural community, creator campaign.

Pitch line:

> One child milestone can invite an entire family graph into Solana.

### 4. Cultural Distribution

The global tooth-tradition library is not just content. It is a localization engine.

Pitch line:

> Every culture has its own doorway into the same Network.

### 5. AI Filter Moat

If the upgraded AI filter lands, it helps show that TFN is not a generic minting flow. The child makes a creative keepsake in a story-world style.

Pitch line if ready:

> Children do not just upload a picture. They choose a Network style tied to the stories of the world.

### 6. Solana Mobile Proof

If ready, this is a clean "consumer Solana" demo point.

Pitch line if ready:

> TFN is built for the kind of warm consumer moment Solana Mobile needs: a parent and child, a phone, and a real on-chain action.

### 7. Revenue Without Overclaiming

The business model should sound plausible without fake precision.

Best staged model:

- Deposit fee on family gifts.
- Premium family memory features.
- Creator/culture campaigns.
- Dental/family partnerships.
- Optional future yield/stablecoin products after audit and consent.

Avoid:

- Over-indexing on "20 teeth per child."
- Sounding like the business depends on children trading assets.
- Making regulatory-heavy claims about investments.

## Recommended Pitch Additions

Add these three ideas to the pitch:

1. One child milestone can invite a whole family graph into Solana.
2. The gift stays on Solana instead of being a temporary checkout event.
3. Culture and AI filters turn the product into a story-world, not a commodity mint flow.

## What To Leave Out Unless Asked

- Detailed revenue projections.
- Yield split mechanics.
- Long father/origin lore.
- Deep regulatory discussion.
- Too much competitor comparison.
