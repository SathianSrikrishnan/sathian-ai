# Tanda Avatar Production Brief

Goal: make Tanda credible as the CEO of Tooth Fairy Network while preserving the exact young, polished Tanda identity from the current renders.

## Production Decision

Use a hybrid avatar format:

- Tanda delivers the pitch as the CEO-host.
- Use short talking-avatar segments, not one uninterrupted 3-minute talking head.
- Product screen recordings carry the proof.
- Sathian appears only through a few short soundbites or voice lines.

This gives the pitch a memorable character without hiding the real founder or asking a talking-avatar tool to carry too much motion.

## Character Lock

Use the visual rules in `tanda-visual-lock.md`.

Tanda is young, brunette, bright, warm, and polished. She is the Tanda from the current app renders and pose packs. Do not use the older watercolor/silver-haired mythology Tanda for this submission.

## Performance Direction

Tanda is:

- Confident and warm.
- Clear enough for judges.
- Protective of children and families.
- Proud of Solana as the infrastructure behind her network.
- A little magical, but not childish.

She should not sound like:

- A bedtime narrator.
- A crypto hype announcer.
- A mascot.
- A parody CEO.

## Avatar Clip List

1. Opening: "I am Tanda, CEO of the Tooth Fairy Network."
2. Problem: "Families are more spread out than ever, but childhood milestones still bring everyone together."
3. Product: "We turn a lost tooth into a permanent keepsake and a family savings ritual."
4. Solana: "The magic leads. Solana keeps the value, the proof, and the action alive underneath."
5. Proof: "This is already running on Solana mainnet."
6. Founder handoff: "Sathian built this as a father first, then as a founder."
7. Market: "Every culture has a tooth story. Every family needs a way to show up."
8. Close: "The tooth fairy just got an upgrade."

## Still Requirements For Talking Avatar

The talking-avatar still should be:

- Portrait or waist-up.
- Face forward or very slight three-quarter angle.
- Mouth visible and neutral-smiling.
- Eyes looking toward camera.
- Hair, dress, satchel, and wings consistent with `tanda-v2-reference.jpg`.
- Background soft and simple.
- No text, no logo, no app UI.

## First Avatar Test

Script:

"I am Tanda, CEO of the Tooth Fairy Network. We turn a lost tooth into a keepsake, a family gift, and a child's first Solana savings story."

Target:

- 9 to 12 seconds.
- 16:9 version for pitch.
- 9:16 optional for social clips.

Acceptance:

- Face still looks like Tanda.
- Mouth movement is acceptable.
- Eyes do not drift.
- Wings do not flicker or cross the face.
- Voice feels warm, confident, and judge-friendly.
