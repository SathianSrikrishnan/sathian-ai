# Pitch Script V0

Target: 2:45 to 2:58 at roughly 145 to 155 words per minute.
Speaker: Tanda, CEO of the Tooth Fairy Network.

## Draft

I am Tanda, CEO of the Tooth Fairy Network.

For centuries, a lost tooth has done something powerful. It brings a family together around a tiny milestone. A child smiles. A parent celebrates. Someone leaves a gift.

But families are more spread out than ever. Grandparents live in different cities. Parents co-parent across households. People who love a child are not always in the same room when the milestone happens.

Tooth Fairy Network gives that family network one place to show up.

When a child loses a tooth, a parent opens toothfairy.network, captures the smile, or lets the child draw the tooth. That moment becomes a permanent digital keepsake. Then the family can add a gift through a shareable link: parents, grandparents, co-parents, aunts, uncles, anyone who wants to be part of the story.

Those gifts can stay on Solana in escrow until the child grows up.

The magic leads. Solana keeps the value, proof, and action alive underneath.

Compressed NFTs make keepsakes cheap enough for a consumer family product. Arweave keeps the memory permanent. An Anchor program holds family deposits without Tooth Fairy Network custodying the funds. Blinks and mobile wallet flows make the ritual portable.

This is already live on mainnet. The program is deployed. The Merkle tree is live. Keepsakes display in Phantom. Gift links and Solana-native deposits are part of the flow.

Sathian built this as a father first, then as a founder. He is building for his daughters, Isa and Sia, and for parents who want a child's first digital asset to feel like love, not speculation.

Our go-to-market is direct and creator-led: parents, family influencers, cultural tooth-story videos, Solana Mobile, and the Solana community itself.

This is not just a tooth fairy app. It is a new consumer doorway into Solana, built from a ritual families already understand.

Tooth Fairy Network turns family milestones into sticky Solana accounts.

The tooth fairy just got an upgrade.
