# Founder Soundbites

These are the only Sathian recordings the pitch likely needs. Keep each one short and human.

## Soundbite 1 - Why This Exists

Target: 10 to 12 seconds.

"I started Tooth Fairy Network as a dad. I wanted my daughters' first digital asset to feel like a family memory, not a crypto product."

## Soundbite 2 - Why Solana

Target: 8 to 10 seconds.

"Solana makes this practical: fast enough for a child, cheap enough for families, and powerful enough to hold real savings."

## Soundbite 3 - What I Need Next

Target: 8 to 12 seconds.

"Now I need parents, creators, and the Solana community to try it, share it, and help make this a real family network."

## Recording Notes

- Record on phone or webcam in a quiet room.
- Smile before each line.
- Leave one second of silence before and after each take.
- Do 3 takes of each line.
