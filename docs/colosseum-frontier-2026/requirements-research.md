## Judging Criteria

The official rules list these project judging criteria:

- Functionality: how well the project works and the quality of the code.
- Potential impact: total addressable market and impact on the Solana ecosystem.
- Novelty: uniqueness of the concept.
- UX: how well the product uses Solana's performance to create strong downstream user experience.
- Open source: whether the project is open source and composes with Solana ecosystem primitives.
- Business plan: whether a viable business can be built around the submission.

Implication for TFN: the pitch cannot be only charming. It must explicitly land the business, Solana UX, and open-source/composability case. The technical video must show real implementation choices.

## Pitch Video Guidance

Colosseum's submission workshop says the pitch video is usually the first item judges review and is the most important element of the submission. It should be no more than 3 minutes and include:

- Team background.
- The problem being solved.
- Who the product is for.
- User feedback or validation, even informal.
- Broader vision.

They also advise treating it like a brief startup pitch, not a product demo. Clear narrative matters more than fancy editing.

Source:
- https://blog.colosseum.com/perfecting-your-hackathon-submission/

## Technical Demo Guidance

The technical demo should be 2 to 3 minutes and should not become another pitch. It should cover:

- Design and implementation choices.
- How the product leverages Solana.
- Core features built.
- Tech stack.
- Architecture.
- Reasoning behind technical decisions, especially Solana integration and on-chain logic.

Source:
- https://blog.colosseum.com/perfecting-your-hackathon-submission/

## What Strong Pitches Include

Colosseum's strategy guide says effective final presentations include:

- Team background.
- Product description.
- Why the team started building it.
- Potential market opportunity unlocked by the product.
- How initial product usage will happen, or existing traction and feedback.
- How the product works.

It also says presentations are required to be under 3 minutes.

- https://blog.colosseum.com/how-to-win-a-colosseum-hackathon/
