# Business Positioning

## The Real Market Frame

Tooth Fairy Network is not mainly a "20 teeth per child" business. The stronger frame is that a lost tooth is a recurring family ritual that opens a trusted financial relationship.

The world is more distributed now. Grandparents live in different cities. Parents co-parent across households. Aunts, uncles, godparents, and close friends want to show up for a child even when they are not physically there. Tooth Fairy Network gives that family network one simple place to gather around a milestone and put a little value behind the love.

## Solana Ecosystem Value

TFN is useful to Solana because it creates a reason for non-trader families to enter and stay:

- It brings parents into Solana through a familiar ritual, not speculation.
- It creates youth-facing financial literacy without telling children to trade.
- It keeps deposits in Solana accounts instead of making crypto a temporary checkout step.
- It gives Solana Mobile and Phantom a warm consumer use case beyond DeFi.
- It creates repeatable on-chain actions: keepsakes, deposits, claims, family links, Blinks, and mobile wallet flows.
- It can invite an entire family graph into Solana from one child's milestone.
- It gives the child a creative reason to participate: a handmade drawing becomes a polished magical keepsake before the on-chain proof appears underneath.

The strongest Colosseum line: TFN turns a child's magical milestone into a sticky Solana account the whole family can gather around.

## Go-To-Market

Near-term GTM should be framed as direct, scrappy, and creator-led:

- Put the app in front of parents and family influencers.
- Ask parents to create one keepsake and share the gift link with family.
- Use short videos about global tooth traditions to earn attention.
- Use Tanda as a recognizable character who explains the product without making it feel like crypto.
- Push Solana Mobile Store if ready, because it gives the judges a clean "consumer Solana" proof point.
- Use Magic Studio as the shareable creative moment: preserve the child's drawing, transform it into a keepsake, then let the family link travel.

## Business Model

Use a staged model, not fake precision:

1. Deposit fee: small percentage on family gifts and locked savings.
2. Premium family features: multi-child dashboards, custom keepsake themes, milestone bundles, family memory pages.
3. Magic Studio credits: starter credits are subsidized; paid bundles can arrive after the card/on-ramp path is ready.
4. Creator and influencer campaigns: sponsored launches around family rituals, cultures, and parenting communities.
5. Partnerships: dental offices, pediatric care, schools, family services, and Solana ecosystem distribution.
6. Future yield/stablecoin layer: optional, consent-based savings options once the product is audited and the user base proves demand.

## Business Model Gap Scan

See `business-model-gap-scan.md` for the expanded version. The strongest additions to weave into the pitch are:

- One child milestone can invite a whole family graph into Solana.
- The gift stays on Solana instead of being a temporary checkout event.
- Culture and AI filters make the product a story-world, not a commodity mint flow.
