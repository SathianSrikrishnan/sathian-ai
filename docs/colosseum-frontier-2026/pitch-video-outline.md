# Pitch Video Outline

Target length: 2:45 to 2:58
Format: Tanda as CEO of the Tooth Fairy Network, with app screen share and proof shots.
Tone: magical founder pitch, not a kids commercial.

## Thesis

Every family already understands the ritual of a child losing a tooth. Tooth Fairy Network turns that moment into a permanent keepsake, a family gift link, and a sticky Solana savings account.

## Viewer Shift

Before: this sounds like a cute tooth fairy app.
After: this is a serious consumer onboarding wedge for Solana: a trusted family ritual, distributed-family gifting, global cultural storytelling, and real on-chain savings hidden behind magic.

## Timing

### 0:00 to 0:20 - Tanda Opens the Arena

Visuals:
- Tanda talking/avatar shot.
- Quick montage: child smile, tooth, Tanda, app home.

Message:
- "I am Tanda, CEO of the Tooth Fairy Network."
- The tooth fairy ritual is already a family money moment.
- TFN upgrades that moment for the next generation.

### 0:20 to 0:45 - Problem

Visuals:
- Coin under pillow, forgotten memory, piggy bank.
- Parent phone opening app.

Message:
- Families are more distributed now: grandparents, co-parents, relatives, friends, and caregivers are often in different homes and cities.
- Parents still want childhood milestones to feel shared and meaningful.
- Crypto onboarding is too cold and technical for families.
- The first wallet needs to feel like care, not finance.

### 0:45 to 1:25 - Product

Visuals:
- Visit toothfairy.network.
- Capture/draw a tooth.
- Magic Studio turns the child's drawing into a polished keepsake while preserving the original hand.
- Keepsake preview.
- Mint/proof.
- Gift page.

Message:
- Child loses a tooth.
- Child draws the memory and Magic Studio creates the wow moment.
- Parent saves a permanent keepsake.
- Family members can share it first, then add gifts through the link from anywhere.
- Savings can be locked until the child grows up.

### 1:25 to 1:55 - Why Solana

Visuals:
- Phantom proof.
- cNFT/Merkle tree proof.
- Blink or transaction proof.
- Short architecture visual.

Message:
- Solana makes the magic instant and cheap.
- Compressed NFTs make many childhood milestones practical.
- Escrow gives the family a trustless savings primitive.
- Blinks/share links turn family distribution into a native Solana loop.
- The result keeps family value inside Solana instead of treating crypto as a temporary checkout step.

### 1:55 to 2:25 - Proof and Founder-Market Fit

Visuals:
- Mainnet contract.
- App proof clips.
- Tanda/world stories.
- Sathian/family reference only if desired.

Message:
- Built by Sathian, a dad building for his daughters.
- Mainnet program is live.
- cNFTs display in Phantom.
- Magic Studio, gift links, mobile flow, and Solana-native deposit path are live or staged.
- Solana Mobile Store deployment is a near-term proof point if ready before submission.

### 2:25 to 2:50 - Market and Business

Visuals:
- 20 teeth per child.
- 105 global traditions.
- Family graph: parents, grandparents, aunts/uncles.
- Dental/school/family services hint.

Message:
- The market starts with a universal ritual and expands through parents, creators, family influencers, cultural stories, and paid Magic Studio credits.
- Every culture has a tooth story, and every distributed family needs simple ways to show up.
- Revenue starts with deposit fees and expands into premium family features, creator campaigns, and family/dental partnerships.

### 2:50 to 2:58 - Close

Visuals:
- Tanda returns.
- App URL and final proof.

Message:
- "The tooth fairy just got an upgrade."
- "Tooth Fairy Network is the first children's product on Solana built from a ritual every family already understands."

## Must-Have Lines

- "This is not a fintech app for kids. It is a cultural protocol for family savings."
- "The magic leads. Solana keeps the value, proof, and action alive underneath."
- "The child's drawing is the emotional asset. AI makes it magical; Solana makes it durable."
- "Sathian built this as a father first, then as a founder."
- "TFN turns family milestones into sticky Solana accounts."

## Avoid

- Do not spend the pitch proving every instruction in the contract.
- Do not say "NFT" too early; say "digital keepsake" first.
- Do not let Tanda sound childish. She is magical, but she is the CEO.
