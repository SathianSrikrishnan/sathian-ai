# Tanda Visual Lock

Status: corrected on 2026-05-06 after Sathian review.

## Canonical Tanda For Colosseum

Use Tanda: the young, bright, polished Tanda from the current app renders.

Naming note: the approved working full name is Tanda Faye. Use "Tanda" in most public pitch copy and "Tanda Faye" in deeper lore/character materials.

She has:

- Warm brunette wavy hair.
- Large expressive brown eyes.
- Youthful face and friendly smile.
- White layered dress.
- Small leather satchel.
- Pastel iridescent fairy wings.
- Soft cinematic 3D / polished character-render look.
- Confident, helpful CEO energy.

She is not:

- Old.
- Silver-haired.
- Cropped-haired.
- Weathered watercolor.
- A dark mythological version.
- A generic Ghibli/storybook fairy.
