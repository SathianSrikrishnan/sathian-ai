## Style Lock

- Polished 3D animated family-film quality, warm character acting, cinematic rooftop light, sculpted expressive faces, crisp silhouettes, premium toy-like material finish.
- Do not use watercolor, flat storybook wash, pastel paper texture, sketch lines, grainy painterly texture, or generic fantasy-bird styling.
- South Korean domestic details should feel lived-in and specific: room textures, roofline, grandmother's tray, phone recording, folded bedding, tile patterns.
- Kkachi must read as a Korean magpie guardian, not a generic magical bird: black head and body, white wing/belly areas, long tail, iridescent blue-green feather edges, sharp comic posture.
- Tanda is physically present but secondary after her introduction. Do not include her as a mysterious background watcher in frames 01-13.
- Avoid text in images except abstract song lines, roofline echo marks, or faint musical staff-like charm lines.
- Visible Korean chant wording or Hangul must stay out of generated art until language review approves exact wording.
- Final frame art must be generated as individual 16:9 landscape images, one image per frame. Do not use contact sheets or multi-panel boards for final storybook deployment.
- Tanda must remain the established adult tooth fairy: human-like fairy woman, translucent glowing wings, soft white dress, tooth pendant, warm golden Toothlight, no animal features, no panda/bear/mascot body, no plush toy face.

## Character Anchors

- Kkachi: elegant, gossipy, precise about rhythm, expressive head tilts, bright eye glints, talon taps like a conductor.
- Jiyoon: age 6-8, modern home clothes, loose hair or simple clips, earnest performer energy that softens into listening.
- Grandmother: warm, patient, grounded, a real person with humor and memory; no shorthand costume or caricature.
- Tanda: adult, calm Toothlight presence, attentive eyes, restrained gestures, learns by listening before touching the moment with magic.
