# Kkachi and the Roof Song - Frame Prompt Pack

Use these prompts for the final 45-frame visual run. Match the approved references in `reference/approved-pilot-board-v1.png` and `reference/approved-home-sequence-board-v1.png`.

Global style for every frame: individual 16:9 landscape production image, polished 3D animated family-film storybook look, playful sculpted characters, cinematic lighting, expressive faces, crisp silhouettes, rich depth of field, premium toy-like material finish, luminous rooftop blue, iridescent feather green, warm Toothlight gold, soft Jiyoon rose, and grandmother warmth. No watercolor, no flat illustration, no pastel paper wash, no sketch texture, no grainy painterly finish, no generic fantasy bird, no multi-panel contact sheet.

Continuity locks:

- Jiyoon is a Korean child age 6-8 in cozy modern home clothes with subtle rose accents.
- Grandmother is a warm Korean elder in contemporary home clothing, patient and specific, never caricatured.
- Kkachi is a Korean magpie guardian with black head/body, white wing and belly areas, long tail, iridescent blue-green feather edges, bright comic eyes, and conductor-like talon gestures.
- Tanda is absent before Frame 14. After introduction, she is physically present but secondary, listening before she uses Toothlight. Match the established adult Tanda reference from Story 2: human-like adult tooth fairy woman, warm brown hair, translucent glowing wings, soft white layered dress, small tooth pendant, warm golden Toothlight, graceful and calm. No panda, no bear, no animal features, no mascot, no plush toy face.
- Do not include readable Korean text, Hangul, romanized chant text, captions, signs, logos, or UI text in generated images.
