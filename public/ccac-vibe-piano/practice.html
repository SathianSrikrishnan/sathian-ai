For teachers: what this experiment does

MIDI imports provide notes and timing. Choose a short phrase, isolate upper or lower notes, listen slowly, then try Follow mode. Correct keys advance the exercise; other keys leave it waiting. This is a listening and key-finding aid, not a replacement for rhythm, reading, posture or teacher-chosen fingering.

MIDI format 0/1, tempo changes and sustain pedal are supported. Percussion is omitted. Limits: 1 MB, 10,000 notes and 20 minutes. MP3s, videos and sheet-music PDFs need transcription first.
